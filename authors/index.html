How bylines work here

  • One name per piece. Whoever reported and wrote it. Editors who materially rewrote a piece are credited alongside.
  • No anonymous staff bylines. "Staff" and "Newsroom" are used only for compiled or live-updating pages, and the responsible editor is still named in the page metadata.
  • Credentials are stated where they matter. Medical, legal and statistical claims are carried by people with the qualification to make them, and the qualification appears in the byline.
  • Contributors are bound by the same rules as staff — see freelancers and contributors.
  • Conflicts are disclosed on the profile. Employment, consultancy, funding and political roles that touch a beat appear on the author page, not buried in a story.

Reaching a writer

Each profile carries a direct route. For anything time-sensitive, or if the writer is unavailable, use corrections@pulseamericanews.com — it's monitored daily and reaches an editor regardless of who is on shift. Complaints that a writer can't resolve escalate under the complaints process.
