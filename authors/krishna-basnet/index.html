Krishna Basnet

Founder · Technology desk
Kavre, Bagmati, Nepal

Krishna Basnet founded Pulse America News and built the site itself — the front end, the publishing pipeline and the data tooling behind the water and energy trackers. He writes on developer tooling, the web platform and the product decisions that sit underneath consumer software.

He is an IT undergraduate in his eighth semester at Kabhre Multiple Campus, Tribhuvan University, and a self-taught developer. In December 2025 he built and shipped Coodeverse, a free browser-based coding platform with live multi-language compilers and no sign-up wall — the same no-friction principle now applied to how this site loads.

Before code, competitive taekwondo: gold medal at the South Asian Taekwondo Championship in July 2018, and an ITF-accredited Asian-level umpire. Both are decision-making under a clock, which is roughly what a publishing deadline is.
