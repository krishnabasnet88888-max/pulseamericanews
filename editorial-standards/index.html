Editorial standards

Rules we hold ourselves to, published so they can be checked against the work.

Source hierarchy

First: primary documents — statutes, dockets, orders, filings, datasets, peer-reviewed papers. Second: named practitioners and institutional experts on the record. Third: journalism. A claim resting only on the third tier is labelled as such.

Numbers

Every figure carries attribution. If no source exists, the figure is written as unknown rather than estimated. Arithmetic we perform ourselves is shown so a reader can repeat it.

Unnamed sources

Used only where a named source would be exposed to retaliation, and the reason is stated in the sentence.

Second-hand figures

Where a number comes from an aggregator rather than the primary document, the page says so explicitly.

Corrections

Errors are corrected in place with a dated note at the foot of the article. The process is set out on the corrections page.

Updates

Revised articles carry a visible update line and a changed dateModified.

AI policy

Articles are drafted with AI assistance and edited by a human. Every claim is checked against the source before publication. Where a piece contains no first-hand reporting, it says "we did not test this" in the method box.

Advertising

Display advertising is separated from editorial. Sponsored material is labelled.
