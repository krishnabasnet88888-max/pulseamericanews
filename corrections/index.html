Corrections

If we get something wrong, we want to know before you tell anyone else.

How to report one

Email corrections@pulseamericanews.com with the URL, the sentence, and the source that contradicts it. A document is better than an assertion.

What we do

  • Factual errors are corrected in place, with a dated note at the foot of the article.
  • Headline changes that alter meaning are noted the same way.
  • Where a correction changes the argument, the article is re-edited rather than patched.
  • Nothing is quietly deleted. Superseded text is described, not removed.

Log
