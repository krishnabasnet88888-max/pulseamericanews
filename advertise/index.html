Advertise with us

We're small and we know it. What we offer instead of reach is a specific audience, no programmatic junk, and a price a small budget can actually reach.

This is a direct-sold inventory page. If you're looking for a million impressions, we'll be honest early and you should probably go elsewhere. If you want to reach people reading about water policy, grid capacity or housing, and you want your banner where a human chose to put it, read on.

Who they are, roughly: policy and utility professionals, developers and infrastructure people, climate and water researchers, and general readers following the drought and housing beats. Formats

Display — direct

Leaderboard above the fold, in-article rectangle, sidebar. Served direct, so it's one ad on the page, not nine. Static image or single HTML5 unit — no autoplay, no sound.

$18 / 1,000 impressions · $250 minimum

Newsletter sponsorship

One sponsor per send, top slot, 60 words plus a link. Sent to the full list at 7 AM ET. Sold per send or as a four-send block.

$180 per send · $600 for four

Sponsored article

700–1,000 words, written to our house style, labelled "Sponsored" at the top and excluded from editorial feeds. One revision included; we fact-check the client's claims.

$450 · $250 writing fee if you supply copy

Section sponsorship

A single section — water, energy or housing — badged "Supported by you" for a calendar month, with a display unit and newsletter mention.

$400 / month · one client per section

Bundle discounts: 10% on two formats, 15% on three or more. Rates are in USD, exclude tax, and are reviewed quarterly.

What advertisers don't get

  • No copy approval. Sponsored pieces are fact-checked with you, but the final wording is ours. You may pull out before publication; you may not rewrite after.
  • No placement inside editorial. Sponsored units sit in defined slots. We won't wrap a story around an ad.
  • No category exclusivity unless you buy section sponsorship for that month.
  • No guarantee of an editorial mention. Buying an ad does not get you covered. It never has.

Full labelling and separation rules are in our editorial standards.

Reporting

You get a monthly summary: impressions served, clicks, click-through rate, and newsletter opens and clicks for sponsorships. Numbers come from our own analytics and the mail platform.

We report what happened rather than what it meant. We don't claim conversion attribution, because we can't see your checkout.

How it works

  1. You email advertise@pulseamericanews.com with what you want to reach and a rough budget.
  2. We reply within three working days with availability and a quote — or say plainly if we're a poor fit.
  3. You approve, we invoice 50% up front, balance on the first day of the run. Payment before the first impression, on first bookings.
  4. Creative in, campaign runs, monthly report out.
